Every portfolio I've built had the same quiet problem: it only showed finished things. But most of what I actually do is learn — and since AI became part of my daily workflow, the rate of learning went up while the rate of *publishing* went down. Finished posts are heavy; learning is light.

So the 2027 version of this site flips the model. The **log** is the centerpiece: short entries, written in minutes, about one thing I learned. Entries marked with ✦ were drafted with an AI model and edited by me — and every one of them shows the actual prompt that started it. No pretending the machine didn't help. No pretending I didn't.

Longer essays live in Writing, co-written the same way, with a "how this was made" box.

This is the experiment: learn in public, credit the tooling, keep the human in the loop.
