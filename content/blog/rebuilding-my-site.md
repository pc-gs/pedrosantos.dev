I've had a portfolio site for a while. It looked fine — dark mode, smooth animations, the whole scroll-through-resume experience. But it felt like everyone else's.

## The problem with template portfolios

The pattern is familiar: hero with your title, a grid of technology logos, project cards with placeholder images, tabs for experience and certifications. It checks every box and says almost nothing specific about you.

I wanted something that reads more like a builder's home base — direct, content-first, and honest about where I am right now.

## What I'm aiming for

A few principles for the rebuild:

- **Engineer-first** — lead with what I build, not founder titles
- **Multi-page** — projects and blog get their own URLs, worth sharing
- **Static for now** — no live feeds or AI chat (yet)
- **Melro.io as a project** — proud of it, but not the headline

## What's next

This is v1. I'll add more project case studies, start writing regularly, and eventually explore an AI layer — a chat or command palette over my own content. But first: ship something real and iterate from there.

If you're reading this, the rebuild is live. Let me know what you think.
